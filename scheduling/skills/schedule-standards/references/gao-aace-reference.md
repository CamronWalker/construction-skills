# GAO & AACE Schedule Standards Reference

## GAO Schedule Assessment Guide (GAO-16-89G)

The GAO guide organizes schedule quality into four characteristics. It is the gold standard for public projects and provides a more comprehensive framework than the DCMA 14-point alone.

### 1. Comprehensive
The schedule should capture all required work: procurement, submittals, permits, inspections, owner decisions, commissioning, punchlist, and weather/seasonal considerations. Every deliverable in the contract should trace to schedule activities.

### 2. Well-Constructed
Sound scheduling practices: complete logic, appropriate relationship types, minimal constraints, no negative lag, reasonable durations, consistent calendars, proper activity types, and a WBS that supports reporting. The network should produce a realistic critical path without manual overrides.

### 3. Credible
Realistic, defensible dates: rational critical path, reasonable float values, durations aligned with historical data, and risk acknowledged. The schedule should be achievable under normal conditions and defensible if challenged.

### 4. Controlled
Consistent maintenance: regular updates, current data date, actual dates recorded, baseline maintained, changes documented, Baseline Execution Index (BEI) and Critical Path Length Index (CPLI) tracked. A controlled schedule provides a reliable basis for performance measurement.

## AACE Recommended Practices

AACE International publishes detailed recommended practices for specific scheduling topics:

### RP 29R-03 — Forensic Schedule Analysis
Methods for analyzing schedule delays after the fact. Covers contemporaneous period analysis, collapsed as-built, impacted as-planned, and time impact analysis (TIA) techniques. Essential for delay claims and dispute resolution.

### RP 38R-06 — Documenting the Schedule Basis
Framework for documenting assumptions, constraints, risks, and methodology behind a schedule. The Schedule Basis document accompanies the schedule and provides context that the CPM network alone cannot convey.

### RP 49R-06 — Identifying the Critical Path
Techniques for identifying and validating the critical path, including longest path analysis, near-critical path analysis, and multiple float path analysis. Addresses common issues like constraint-driven critical paths and calendar-induced anomalies.

### RP 53R-06 — Schedule Update Review
Checklist and methodology for reviewing schedule updates. Covers data date verification, progress recording, logic changes, added/deleted activities, and trending analysis.

## Organizational Standards Layer

The industry standards above form the foundation. Organizations layer on additional requirements:

### Common Organizational Additions
- **WBS Standards** — Required levels, naming conventions, mandatory nodes
- **Activity Standards** — ID format, naming conventions, duration ranges, required codes
- **Logic Standards** — Density ratio (1.5-2.5), maximum chain length, milestone gates
- **Calendar Standards** — Standard definitions, holidays, seasonal adjustments
- **Update Standards** — Frequency, required fields, baseline rules, narrative requirements

Westland's specific organizational standards are documented in `westland-standards.md` in this references directory.
