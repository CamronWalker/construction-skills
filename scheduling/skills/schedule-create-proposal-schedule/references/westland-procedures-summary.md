# Westland Scheduling Procedures — Proposal-Stage Summary

Distilled from `Westland Scheduling Procedures Outline - 2026-03-09.pdf` (same folder). Scoped to what a **proposal schedule** needs. Skip the Schedule Maintenance & Updates, TIA/Fragnet, and SmartPM sections when working at this stage — those are post-award.

## Purpose Reminder

A proposal schedule is a **sales document built to win the job**. A reviewer will compare it against competitors' schedules. Clean, credible, defensible wins; cosmetically high-scoring with obvious metric-gaming loses.

## Philosophy

- **Template-based, not boilerplate.** Start from a Westland template or similar completed project. Adapt to the specific project. "Activities, logic, and durations for each schedule must be unique. However, the basic structure, organization, and workflow should be consistent across all Westland projects."
- **Be disciplined up front.** Truly digest the documents and understand the project before generating.
- **Consult trade partners, suppliers, design teams when you can.** At proposal stage this is often limited — use similar projects and historical data as the fallback, and flag what needs real input.
- **Resist the temptation to assume the schedule is accurate.** "It may be a great outline, but without real input from trade partners, suppliers, design teams, owners, etc. the schedule will not be a true reflection of how the work will progress." This framing drives the Phase 7 anti-score-chasing discipline: honesty beats metric optimization.

## Conceptual / Proposal Schedule Checklist

From the procedures doc's "Develop Conceptual Project Schedule with Project Proposal" bullets. Every Phase 3 output must address each item:

1. **Westland template or Westland standards** for organization and formatting.
2. **Design & permitting schedule from the design team** — for CM/GC or Design-Build contracts include:
   - Schematic Design
   - Design Development
   - 70% Construction Documents
   - 100% Construction Documents
   - Permit / Consent Process
   Include the design process in the conceptual schedule with durations from the design team if available, or placeholder durations flagged "needs design team input" otherwise.
3. **Preconstruction deliverables** — coordinate with estimating / preconstruction for appropriate detail and durations:
   - Prequalification
   - Buy out / Proposal & Award
   - Estimates / Constructability Reports / Schedule Updates
   - BIM Modeling (if applicable)
4. **Procurement section** for key materials:
   - Identify materials needing early release, expedited reviews, or careful monitoring due to market issues
   - Include all long-lead items and materials likely to experience delays
   - Each long-lead gets **BOTH a fabrication activity AND a shipping activity** with valid durations
5. **Site Logistics & Workflow Plan prior to the detailed schedule** — a graphic zone-and-arrow plan view showing anticipated scope, areas, zoning, phasing, and flow. See the Lubumbashi/Querétaro temple examples in the PDF. If one has not been developed, flag it as a Phase 4 question to the PMT rather than inferring zones from the sample schedule.
6. **Detailed construction schedule** with typical activities and durations for the proposed building.
7. **Level of detail corresponds to available design info.** Consult team members, reference similar projects, use reasonable assumptions and historical data to compensate for incomplete design.
8. **Westland standard responsibility codes on every task.** No unassigned activities.

## Standard Westland WBS Template

Reproduced verbatim from p.5/p.11 of the procedures PDF. The canonical starting tree — `wbs-patterns.md` adapts it for phasing variants (greenfield, occupied rebuild, phased turnover).

```
PROJECT
  SUMMARY & MILESTONES
    CONTRACT MILESTONES & SUMMARY BARS
    KEY PERFORMANCE MILESTONES

  PRE-CONSTRUCTION
    DESIGN
      ESTIMATES - CONSTRUCTABILITY REPORTS - SCHEDULE UPDATES
      BIM MODELING
      TRADE PRE-QUALIFICATION
      BUY OUT - PROPOSAL & AWARD
      OWNER PERMIT / CONSENT PROCESS

  PROCUREMENT
    SUBMITTALS - APPROVALS - FABRICATION - DELIVERY

  CONSTRUCTION
    SITEWORK
      INITIAL SITEWORK
        CLEAR & GRUB - CUT & FILL - ROUGH GRADE - UTILITIES
      BALANCE OF SITEWORK
        HARDSCAPE - LANDSCAPE - EQUIPMENT ENCLOSURES
    STRUCTURE & SUBROUGH
      AREA - LEVEL
    BUILDING ENCLOSURE - WINDOWS - ENTRIES - FINISH SYSTEMS
      ELEVATION - AREA - LEVEL
    INTERIOR ROUGH-IN & FINISHES
      AREA - LEVEL

  COMMISSIONING & CLOSE-OUT  (may be shown by building, area, or floor)
```

## Minimum Elements at Proposal Stage

Filtered from the procedures-doc minimum-standard list (p.6/p.11-12) to proposal-relevant items only:

- **Milestones — one every 30 days** on the critical path or for significant events. Include Westland standard required milestones plus any required by the project specifications.
- **Prequalification Process / Mock-Ups**
- **Design phases** (if CM/GC or Design-Build): SD, DD, 70% CD, 100% CD
- **Submittals / Procurement / Mock-Ups**
- **Preconstruction Activities**
- **Construction phases:**
  - Sitework
  - Structure & Sub-Rough
  - Building Enclosure
  - Interior Rough-In & Finishes
- **Closeout**

**Out of scope for a proposal:**
- Progress Impact buckets (Weather Days / PRs-ASIs / Owner Delays / Contractor Delays) — construction-phase tracking, not sales material. A proposal that includes them looks like a half-filled-in baseline rather than a clean commitment.
- SmartPM baseline uploads — post-award.
- TIA / Fragnet / IMPACT activities — post-award.
- Weekly update deliverables and 4-week rolling schedules — post-award.
